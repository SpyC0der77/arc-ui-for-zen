# Installer mark

The Zennia mark is original artwork made for this project: a petal-shaped trefoil ribbon knot that combines a zinnia's petals with a woven zen knot. Its geometry is generated, not traced from another logo. Its flat colours and over/under colouring follow the style of the Arc browser icon; no Arc or Zen logo geometry is used.

The construction script and design explorations are kept outside the installer package.
